Our Story

From a small idea to a platform loved by millions.

We started with a simple belief

ShopNest was founded in 2020 by a small team of fashion lovers and developers who were tired of compromising on quality, price, or experience.

Today we serve over 2 million customers across India with hand-picked fashion, electronics, and lifestyle products. Every item passes our quality check before it reaches you.

Our goal is straightforward — make great shopping accessible and enjoyable for everyone, from Mumbai to Manipur.
